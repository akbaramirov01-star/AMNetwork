Rules for charities and donors

Last updated: 24 November 2026

AM Network connects people in need with those who give Zakat and Sadaqah — charities and private donors in any country. These rules apply to every giver who uses the cabinet. They are short, so please read them all.

1. Who can join

Charities and private donors from any country. Sign up at amnetwork.io/cabinet and, under Profile, enter the name of your organisation — or “private donor”.

AM Network approves every giver by hand. We may refuse a request to join, or remove access, at any time.

2. What you see

  • Requests without names, phone numbers, emails or addresses.
  • Each request is labelled “Not verified by AM Network”. We have not checked it — you do.
  • An AI hint: which Zakat category the request may fit and what to check. It is a hint, not a fatwa. A person decides.

3. You verify

Before you help, verify the request yourself: ask for documents, call, visit, or ask a local mosque or community leader who knows the person.

Whether someone is eligible for Zakat is your responsibility. If you are unsure, ask a scholar.

4. Helping

  • Press “I want to help” on the request.
  • AM Network connects you with the applicant only if they agreed to be contacted.
Help goes directly from you to the person. Money never passes through AM Network, and AM Network takes nothing.

5. Never

These are never allowed:

  • Asking the applicant for money, fees or favours.
  • Sharing their data with anyone.
  • Using their data for marketing or for anything other than helping them.
  • Pressuring them.
  • Publishing their photos or story without their written consent.

6. Data

Keep the applicant's contact details only as long as you need them to help, then delete them.

Report any problem — a suspicious request, a data leak, a giver breaking these rules — to contact@amnetwork.io.

7. Breaking the rules

A giver who breaks these rules loses access to AM Network.

8. Contact

Questions about these rules: contact@amnetwork.io
