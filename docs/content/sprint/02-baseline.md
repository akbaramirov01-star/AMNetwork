# Baseline — YouTube Studio export, 28 Aug – 26 Nov 2026 (founder, 26 Nov). Raw files in `data/`.
## Channel, 90 days
- Views 56,628 · watch time **115.8 h** · subscribers +1,372 · thumbnail impressions 23,796 · CTR **2.05%** · 56 videos.
- YPP long-form path needs 4,000 public watch hours/12 months → we are at ~3% (115.8 h in 90 days).
- **Two videos = 85% of all views:** Ayyub #12 (38,737 views, 56.2 h) and Ibrahim #7 "Thrown into fire" (9,647, 31.2 h).
## YPP status (Studio → Monetisation, data as of 21 Nov 2026 — founder screenshots)
- **Qualifying public watch hours (365 days): 1** of 3,000 / 4,000. **Qualifying Shorts views (90 days): 3.2k** of 3M / 10M.
- Owner confirmed (26 Nov): everything except ~1 hour came from paid ads, which do not count. Organic baseline ≈ zero.
- So every sprint test is measured from zero organic; the goal is the first organic watch hours from long-form.

## Paid vs organic (owner confirmed: ads ≈ all of the 115.8 h)
- Ayyub: 2–6k views/day 11–22 Nov, then **10 / 4 / 2 views/day from 23 Nov** — a cliff like this is what an ad campaign
  ending looks like; only 515 thumbnail impressions for 38,737 views and only 11 subscribers → almost certainly paid.
- Ibrahim #7: a steady 140–560 views/day for 5+ weeks (19 Oct → 25 Nov) — either a long-running ad or our one evergreen
  video. **Owner: which videos were promoted, and when?**
- Subscribers: +1,372 in 90 days but per-video subscriber gains add up to ~60 → most subscribers came from ads, not videos.
## Organic reality (unpromoted videos)
- Typical Short: 7–45 views. Typical long (2:30–3:00) episode: 28–146 views.
- Best organic CTRs (small samples): Zamzam re-upload 7.08% (424 impr.), WYWD #1 wallet 8.33% (36), Sulayman v2 6.25% (112),
  Exam Short 5.38%, Ayyub 4.66%. Worst: "The First Human" 0.21% (1,460 impr.), "They CHEERED for this cloud" 0.36%.
- 19-min compilation: 70 views but **7.1 h** → ~6 min average view — the one long video people actually stayed in.
## What this means for the sprint
1. Ads made the numbers look bigger than the organic audience; judge every test on organic videos only.
2. Long-form watch time is the bottleneck for monetisation; Shorts add views but almost no hours.
3. Topic signal worth testing: "patience in loss" (Ayyub) and "miracle under threat" (Ibrahim in the fire) — both
   emotional single-event stories with a clear question in the title.
4. Next data needed: per-video retention and traffic sources (Studio → each video → Analytics), and which posts were boosted.
