## How it works (5 steps)
1. A family applies (or a verifier brings a family it knows).
2. A trained AM Network volunteer visits the family in person and fills a checklist. Whoever brought the family never verifies it.
3. The admin approves help only after the in-person check.
4. The donor gives the Zakat/Sadaqah to the family directly — cash, Alif, Korti Milli or bank transfer. Later: through a registered partner charity.
5. The payout is recorded in the open report (`/transparency/`): amount, date, city/district, situation category, number of checks — no names, phones or addresses. Each entry carries a SHA-256 fingerprint linked to the previous one, so any quiet change breaks the chain; anyone can check it in the browser.
