2026-11-20

A simpler model

AM Network is now a verification and transparency service for Zakat and Sadaqah: volunteers check each family in person, every payout goes into an open report with a SHA-256 hash chain, and the money goes directly from donor to family. Blockchain, token and fee plans were dropped.
