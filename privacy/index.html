Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what data AM Network handles on amnetwork.io — the public pages and free tools, the forms, the AI assistant, the cabinet for families, verifiers, donors and admin, and the open report — who can see it, and how long we keep it. Plain language, no legal filler.

1. Who we are

AM Network is a verification and transparency service for Zakat and Sadaqah. It is not yet a registered legal entity: the project is run by its founder, Akbar Amirzoda, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: contact@amnetwork.io.

Money never passes through AM Network. We do not collect, hold or distribute donations, so we never ask for card numbers or bank account details.

2. Public pages and free tools

You can use the public pages and free tools without an account.

  • Analytics. Google Analytics (GA4) runs only after you accept cookies in the banner; it is off by default. It records pages viewed, approximate location and device type. To withdraw consent, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Settings on your device. Your theme, language and cookie choice are saved in your browser's localStorage. They are not sent to us.
  • Content sources. The Quran, hadith and dua tools load content directly from your browser: the Quran.com API (Quran Foundation) and everyayah.com for the Quran text, translations and recitations; AlAdhan.com for prayer times and the Hijri calendar; the open hadith (fawazahmed0/hadith-api) and dua & dhikr (fitrahive) datasets, served through GitHub and jsDelivr. These services see your IP address and what you requested, under their own privacy policies.
  • Other services. The prayer-times page uses BigDataCloud to turn your location into a city name; the Zakat calculator uses Gold API and Frankfurter for metal prices and exchange rates; the need self-check uses public World Bank country statistics; the live page embeds YouTube; fonts come from Google Fonts and flags from flagcdn.com.
  • Location. Prayer times and Qibla ask for your location only when you allow it. It is used in your browser and sent only to the services that need it to compute the result (AlAdhan, BigDataCloud) — not to us.
  • Calculations. The Zakat calculator and the need self-check run in your browser. Your amounts and answers are not sent to us.
  • Offline use. To work offline, the site keeps copies of its pages in your browser's cache. If you tap “save offline” on a surah, its text, translation and audio are stored on your device too. Clearing this site's data removes them.
  • Friday reminder. If you turn it on (on the prayer-times page), our backend on Render stores your browser's push subscription (an address issued by your browser's push service), your time-zone offset, language and the reminder type. The reminder is delivered through your browser vendor's push service. Turning it off deletes the subscription.

3. Waitlist and public application form

The waitlist form (your email, and name if you give it) and the public application form at /apply/ (contact details, city, household, income, the situation and needs you describe) are sent as an email to our inbox through Web3Forms. hCaptcha checks that a person, not a bot, is sending; it processes interaction signals and your IP address. These submissions are not written to a spreadsheet or database automatically.

An application can contain sensitive information, such as financial hardship or health. Share only what is needed to understand the situation, and only with the consent of the person it is about.

4. AI assistant

  • Questions you tap from the suggested list are answered offline in your browser, from a fixed set of answers. Nothing is sent.
  • A question you type is sent to our backend on Render and from there to Anthropic's API (Claude), which generates the answer. Our backend does not store the text of your questions; to limit abuse it counts requests against a one-way hash of your connection, not your raw IP address. Render may keep short technical logs (such as the IP address and time of a request).
  • Please do not type names, phone numbers, addresses or other personal details into the assistant.
  • The microphone button uses your browser's own speech recognition. Depending on the browser, your voice may be processed by its vendor (for example Google or Apple). Replies are read aloud by your device.

5. Cabinet and open report

Where the data is stored

Data entered in the cabinet (/cabinet/) is stored in Supabase, a database service, in its European region. Who may read each record is decided by rules inside the database itself, not only by the page. The cabinet has no analytics.

What is stored

  • Account: email, password (handled by Supabase's login system; we cannot read it), name, and optionally phone and city; your role (applicant, donor, verifier or admin).
  • Family application: name of the head of the family, phone, city or district, address, household size, number of dependents, monthly income and currency, situation categories, a short description, the family's consent, a reference number (for example AMN-2026-0001) and the status.
  • Verification: date of the visit, the checklist, the need score the checklist adds up (a hint — a person decides), the verifier's recommendation and notes.
  • Payout: amount, currency, Zakat or Sadaqah, method (cash, Alif, Korti Milli, bank transfer or other), date, the donor it is linked to, and the SHA-256 fingerprint of the receipt. The receipt file itself is never uploaded: the fingerprint is computed in your browser.
  • History: a timeline of what happened to an application (created, assigned, checked, approved, paid), by whom and when.

Who sees what

WhoWhat they see
The family (or whoever submitted the application for it)Its own application, status and history
The verifier assigned to the familyThat application, including phone and address, its history, and their own check

DonorTheir own payouts — amount, date, city or district, category, number of checks — without the family's name, phone or address

In a small place, a district and category could help someone who already knows a family guess who it is. That is why the city or district field never asks for a village or street.

Legal basis

Family data is processed with the family's consent, which is recorded with the application. When a volunteer fills in an application for a family, they must get the family's consent beforehand. Account data is processed because you registered and asked to use the cabinet.

6. How we use data

  • To check applications and arrange in-person visits
  • To contact you about your application, your account or the waitlist
  • To publish the open report of payouts
  • To measure and improve the public website (only with analytics consent)

We never sell personal data and never use it for advertising.

8. How long we keep data

  • Cabinet data: while the pilot runs, and as long as it is needed to keep the open report consistent.
  • Open report entries are permanent by design: each is linked to the previous one by its fingerprint, so removing one would break the chain. They contain no personal data.
  • On request we delete your personal data — your account, and a family's name, phone, address and description. We keep only what the open report needs to stay consistent: reference number, city or district, category, amount and date.
  • Form emails stay in our inbox until they are no longer needed or you ask us to delete them. A push subscription is kept until you turn the reminder off.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the data we hold about you
  • Correct it (you can also edit your name, phone and city yourself under Profile in the cabinet)
  • Delete it, as described above
  • Withdraw consent — a family may withdraw at any time; open report entries already published stay, without personal data. Analytics consent is withdrawn by clearing this site's data.

Write to contact@amnetwork.io. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area, these rights are also protected by the GDPR.

10. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Supabase's login system. The database key in the page code is public by design and opens nothing on its own: access is decided by the database rules. The master keys are never put in the website code. No system is perfectly secure, so we collect only what the service needs.

11. Children

AM Network is for adults. Children in a family are only counted (household size and dependents); they are not named. Please do not create an account for a child.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the service changes and change the date at the top. The current version is always on this page.
