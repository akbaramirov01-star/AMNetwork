Simple to give. Life-changing to receive.

Five transparent steps — from your donation to verified delivery.

01

You give

Planned flow: Send zakat or sadaka in any amount. Your funds are locked immediately in a smart contract on Base blockchain — not in our bank account. Full custody to code.

Zakat · Sadaqah
02

AI scores need

Planned flow: Recipients submit encrypted documents. Our AI model scores each case 0–100 based on family size, income level, medical conditions and regional cost of living. Personal data stays off-chain.

AI-Scored
03

Oracle verifies

Planned flow: A trusted local verifier — a certified imam or licensed social worker — reviews the AI assessment and confirms eligibility. For large distributions, the DAO votes.

Human review
04

Automatic transfer

Planned flow: after verification, a smart contract transfers funds from the pool to the recipient's wallet. Security and correct delivery depend on contract audits and recipient verification.

Base · Solana
05

You receive proof

Planned flow: A permanent blockchain record is created. You receive a link showing exactly which verified profiles received your funds, in which countries, on which date — forever on-chain.
