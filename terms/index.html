Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

These terms apply to amnetwork.io: the public pages and free tools, the forms, the AI assistant, the cabinet and the open report. By using the site, you agree to them.

1. What AM Network is

AM Network is a verification and transparency service for Zakat and Sadaqah. We check families in person and publish an open report of every payout. Money never passes through AM Network: we do not collect, hold or distribute funds. The donor gives directly to the family, and we take no fees from Zakat.

The service is pre-launch. A pilot is being prepared in Dushanbe, Tajikistan, with the founder's own and relatives' Zakat. We are not raising money from the public and offer nothing to invest in.

2. No guarantee of help

Sending an application — in the cabinet or through the form at /apply/ — does not guarantee that a family will receive help. Help depends on an in-person check, an admin decision and a donor who chooses to give. Each donor decides and gives on their own responsibility.

3. What a verification means

A verification is an internal check by an AM Network volunteer: a visit, a checklist and a recommendation. The need score is a hint added up from the checklist; a person decides. A verification is not a legal, financial or religious certification of a family, and not a ruling that someone is eligible to receive Zakat.

4. Sharia review

We are seeking a review of our model by a Hanafi scholar before the pilot. AM Network is not certified by any scholar or Sharia board. We will update this page if that changes.

5. Free Islamic tools

The Quran, hadith, dua & dhikr, prayer times, Hijri calendar and other tools show content from the sources named on each page (such as the Quran.com API by Quran Foundation, everyayah.com, AlAdhan.com and the open hadith and dua datasets) as they publish it; we do not edit it. Translations marked as AI-translated are unverified. Prayer times and Qibla depend on your location and chosen method. These tools are informational and do not replace a scholar's guidance.

6. Zakat calculator

The calculator gives an estimate from the amounts you enter, the nisab and current metal prices. It is not a religious ruling. For your own obligation, consult a qualified scholar.

7. AI assistant

Assistant answers, typed or spoken, may contain mistakes, including on religious questions. Check anything important against a primary source or with a scholar. Do not enter personal data.

8. Using the cabinet

If you use the cabinet (/cabinet/), you agree to:

  • Give truthful information, and never use fake documents, receipts or photos
  • Apply on behalf of a family only with its consent, and enter only what it agreed to share
  • As a verifier, visit in person, record only what you saw, act honestly, and never check a family you brought yourself
  • Keep other people's data confidential: do not copy, photograph or share it outside the cabinet
  • Not try to reach data that is not yours, get around the access rules, or use the site for anything unlawful

We may suspend an account that breaks these rules. Knowingly false information may lead to an application being rejected.

9. The open report

Payout entries in the open report (/transparency/) cannot be removed or changed, even on request: each is linked to the previous one by a SHA-256 fingerprint, so any change would be visible. Entries contain no names, phones or addresses; the Privacy Policy lists what they show.

10. No advice, no warranty

Nothing on this site is financial, legal or investment advice. The site and its tools are provided “as is” during a pre-launch phase: they may sometimes be unavailable or inaccurate, and we cannot promise that an application will be reviewed within a set time.

11. Changes

We may update these terms as the service develops; the date at the top shows the current version. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated terms.
