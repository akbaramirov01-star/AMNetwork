About this list
The Prophet ﷺ said Allah has 99 Names, and whoever preserves them will enter Paradise (Sahih al-Bukhari 2736, Sahih Muslim 2677). The specific enumerated list below comes from an addition narrated in Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3507 — scholars note this addition has a weaker chain of transmission than the core hadith, and its exact wording/order varies slightly between sources. Shown here as commonly known, not as a claim of absolute certainty.
