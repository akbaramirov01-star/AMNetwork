FAQ

Common Questions.

Answers to the questions we hear most — how to ask for help, how charities and donors join, what the AI does, who sees your data, fees and Sharia review.

Fill in the form at amnetwork.io/apply — it is free and open to people in any country. Describe your situation in your own language: who is in the family, what happened, what you need. Please do not write phone numbers or emails in the story — contacts go in separate fields, and givers see the request without your name, phone or address. AI reviews the request, approved charities and donors can then see it, and your contacts are shared only if you agree. Applying does not guarantee help, and AM Network never asks you for money. If you need urgent help, please also contact your local mosque or a charity near you.
Open amnetwork.io/cabinet and sign up with your email. In Profile, write the name of your organisation (or that you give privately) and ask to help. AM Network reviews and approves givers by hand. Once approved, you see open requests with their AI review but without names, phones or addresses. Press “I want to help” on a request; with the applicant's consent we connect you, and you verify the request yourself and help directly. Charities and donors from any country are welcome, and it is free.
No. AI reads the request and suggests which of the 8 Zakat categories (Surah At-Tawbah, 9:60) it may fit, which documents to ask for and what looks odd. It is a hint, not a fatwa. A person always decides; if in doubt, ask an imam or a scholar.
For now, the giver: the charity or donor checks documents, calls or visits. Every request carries the label "Not verified by AM Network". AI helps by suggesting the Zakat category, what to check and what raises questions. Later we will have our own verifiers.
Everyone sees only the situation, without name, phone or address. Contact details are shared only with charities and donors we have approved, and only if you agree. We never sell data or use it for advertising.
No one in the middle. AM Network never collects, holds or distributes Zakat. The giver helps the recipient directly — cash, a transfer or through their own charity — and 100% arrives.
