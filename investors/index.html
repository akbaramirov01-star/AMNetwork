The problem

Large potential. Little of it is visible.

Zakat potential is estimated at $50B–$600B a year; under $25B is distributed formally. The rest is given person to person, with no shared way to check who needed it or where it went.

What is missing today

  • Donors rarely know whether anyone has visited a family in person
  • After giving, there is no record that a donor — or anyone else — can look at
  • Families without ties to a fund or a committee are easily missed
  • Without a visible trail, trust in organised giving stays low

What AM Network does

  • A trained volunteer visits every family in person
  • Help is approved only after that visit
  • The donor gives directly to the family — money never passes through us
  • Every payout is recorded in an open, tamper-evident report
How it works

Five steps, from application to public record

1

A family applies

Or a verifier brings a family they know.

2

A volunteer visits in person

A trained AM Network volunteer visits the family and fills a checklist. Whoever brought the family never verifies it.

3

Approval after the visit

The admin approves help only after the in-person check.

4

The donor gives directly

Cash, Alif, Korti Milli or bank transfer — straight to the family. Later: through a registered partner charity.

5

The payout goes into the open report

Amount, date, city/district, situation category and number of checks — no names, phones or addresses.

100% of the Zakat goes from the donor to the family. AM Network never holds or moves the money.

Open report

A record anyone can check

Each entry carries a SHA-256 fingerprint linked to the previous one, so any quiet change breaks the chain. Anyone can check the chain in the browser.

Published for every payout

  • Amount
  • Date
  • City / district
  • Situation category
  • Number of in-person checks

Never published

  • Names
  • Phone numbers
  • Addresses
  • Anything that could identify a family
Why this approach

Simple, checkable, within the rules

🏠

In-person verification

A checklist with visible points (capped at 100) guides the verifier; it is a hint, and a person decides after the visit. Approval needs at least one in-person check, and whoever brought a family can never verify it — both rules are enforced in the database.

🔗

A public report

Donors and the public can see that help reached real, checked situations — without exposing any family.

🤲

No custody

Because we never hold or move money, the pilot should not need a payments or fundraising licence. We are confirming this with a lawyer in Tajikistan before the pilot starts.

⚖️

No fees from Zakat

No income in year one. Later: optional donor tips (separate from Zakat), grants, and service fees that partner charities pay from their own funds.

📚

Scholarly review

We are seeking review of the model by a Hanafi scholar before the pilot.
