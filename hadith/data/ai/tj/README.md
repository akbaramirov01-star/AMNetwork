# Tajik Jami' at-Tirmidhi — AI translation

`tirmidhi.json` maps hadith numbers (as in the fawazahmed0/hadith-api English
edition) to a Tajik (Cyrillic) translation.

- Translated from the English edition by AI (Claude), October 2026, in batches
  of 100 with automatic checks (every source number present, no empty entries,
  no mixed Latin/Cyrillic words). Not paid per view: it is a static file.
- Style follows the published, government-reviewed «Мухтасари Саҳеҳи Бухорӣ»
  (Abdulhalim Orifi, ЭР-граф, 2011): «Аз X (р) ривоят аст…», «Паёмбари Худо (с)».
- Where the English source is truncated or garbled, the translation carries it
  across as written; nothing is invented to fill gaps.
- The reader labels it "AI-translated, not yet reviewed by a scholar". A
  verified published translation must always take priority when available.
- Coverage may be partial while translation is in progress; the reader shows
  English for any number missing here.
